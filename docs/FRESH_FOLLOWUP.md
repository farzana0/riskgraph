# Fresh regression replication

This protocol is registered before paid responses. It fixes 48 new cases (16 each: UTC boundaries, nested reversals and approval totals), drawn from the unchanged hard generator under seed 2026102441. The earlier results select these challenge families; the new responses do not select cases. This is regression enrichment, not a production workload or proof that every new case is harder than every old case.

Every case has Haiku and Sonnet initial proposals with selected memory off/on and a separate Sonnet critic for each valid initial proposal: eight scored configurations, at most 384 requests. The seven actual SurrealDB source snapshots, advice and usage contract from v3 are frozen unchanged. No new retrieval, memory writes, or arithmetic reports enter these requests. This isolates the selected-memory bundle against no memory, not individual retrieval and instruction components. Earlier raw-memory and calculation studies remain separate and are not pooled.

A persisted US$4.80 allowance reserves before every request, retains failed/unknown reserves, reuses completed responses, and forbids retries of failed/uncertain calls. A separate travel experiment and live demo share a US$1.20 allowance: maximum new spend US$6.00. This remains below the stated C$13 credit at the October 23 indicative exchange rate of C$1.3993/US$1, with additional headroom. Auto-reload is on, so the application cap remains binding regardless of provider billing. No account or earlier counter is reset. [Exchange reference](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates-lookup/?dF=2026-10-17&dT=2026-10-24&lP=lookup_daily_exchange_rates_2017.php&rangeType=dates&sR=2017-01-01&se=FXUSDCAD).

The same family Jeffreys frequency, four-band severity, and matched joint Dirichlet model as v3 is used with three equal family weights. Twelve prespecified contrasts cover memory, critic, and model comparisons. Separate 5% frequentist error families cover 24 frequency cells, 36 family/contrast cells (144 endpoints), 16 dollar streams and 12 paired-dollar streams. The four families together are not joint 95% coverage. Fixed-batch exact endpoints require complete outcomes and the stated within-family IID/fixed-policy assumptions. Missing responses retain financial uncertainty and count as service failures.

Balanced prefixes of 4, 8, 12 and 16 cases per family display how marginal Bayesian intervals change as evidence accumulates. They are reuse of the same observations, not independent replications, stopping rules or simultaneous frequentist guarantees. No stopping or favorable-result selection is based on these curves. Budget or provider limits may stop collection early; incomplete cells and pair counts are retained and complete-batch endpoints are withheld.

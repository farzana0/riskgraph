Assumptions, provenance & limits

Each family is a narrow, frozen synthetic distribution with independent case-level random streams. The model assumes a constant Bernoulli failure probability within each scenario/setup. Independent scenario priors support weighted posterior draws; configurations remain paired. Fixed-sample bounds use 0.05 / 24 per cell and a union bound, without requiring independent setups. Guarantees are conditional on the sampling and policy assumptions; they do not validate the scripted agents as real models.

Weights describe an illustrative assumed traffic mix. These projections are not calibrated production risk, actual business loss, annual loss, or CVaR. Source authenticity and real payment infrastructure are outside this simulation. All times in the graph are illustrative replay timings.

Archived real-model outputs are displayed separately. Their four monitoring outcomes are counterfactual deterministic replays, not four new model evaluations. The original critic was advisory; mapping it to a gate is an explicit demo assumption.
